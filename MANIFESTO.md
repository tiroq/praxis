# MANIFESTO

# Praxis Manifesto

> **Turn intent into action.**

---

## Why Praxis Exists

Modern knowledge workers do not suffer from a lack of information.

They suffer from fragmentation.

Ideas appear in conversations.
Tasks appear in messengers.
Meetings produce notes.
Emails contain commitments.
Projects live in GitHub.
Opportunities appear on Upwork.
Plans live in calendars.
Documents are scattered across dozens of systems.

Every tool solves one part of the problem.

None understands the whole.

Praxis exists to connect these pieces into a coherent operating system for work and life.

---

# We Believe

We believe that productivity is not about completing more tasks.

It is about making better decisions.

The quality of our lives is determined by the quality of the decisions we make every day.

Most software helps people remember.

Praxis helps people understand.

---

# Work Is Not a List of Tasks

Traditional productivity software models work as lists.

Tasks.

Projects.

Folders.

Labels.

Reality is different.

Work begins with events.

A conversation.

An idea.

A customer request.

A meeting.

A bug report.

A message.

Every event creates context.

Context creates decisions.

Decisions create actions.

Actions create outcomes.

Praxis models work the way it actually happens.

---

# AI Should Not Replace Judgment

Artificial Intelligence should not become the decision maker.

It should become the best advisor.

Praxis is built around one fundamental principle:

**AI proposes. Humans decide.**

AI may analyze.

AI may summarize.

AI may challenge assumptions.

AI may generate alternatives.

But responsibility always belongs to the human.

---

# Every Decision Should Be Explainable

A recommendation without reasoning is not intelligence.

Praxis never aims to produce mysterious answers.

Every recommendation should explain:

- why it was made;
- which information was considered;
- what assumptions exist;
- what alternatives were rejected.

Understanding is more valuable than automation.

---

# Knowledge Should Compound

Most productivity software stores information.

Praxis connects information.

Every project.

Every client.

Every meeting.

Every proposal.

Every decision.

Every document.

They should become part of a growing knowledge network.

Experience should accumulate instead of disappearing.

---

# Local First

Personal knowledge belongs to its owner.

Whenever practical, processing should happen locally.

Cloud services are integrations, not dependencies.

The platform should remain useful even without external AI providers.

---

# Vendor Independence

Praxis is not tied to a single model.

Nor a single cloud.

Nor a single automation platform.

The best provider today may not be the best tomorrow.

The architecture must make replacing providers easy.

Freedom of choice is a feature.

---

# Composability Over Reinvention

Praxis is not built to replace every existing tool.

It is built to orchestrate them.

Great software already exists.

Calendars.

Git repositories.

Databases.

Automation platforms.

CRMs.

Praxis should integrate them instead of rebuilding them.

Only unique capabilities should be implemented from scratch.

---

# Event Driven

Everything starts with an event.

Events become understanding.

Understanding becomes knowledge.

Knowledge becomes reviews.

Reviews become decisions.

Decisions become actions.

Actions become experience.

Experience improves future decisions.

This cycle is the heart of Praxis.

---

# Human Memory Is Limited

People should not spend their attention remembering what software could remember for them.

Attention is the most valuable resource.

Praxis exists to reduce cognitive overhead.

The goal is not to do more work.

The goal is to spend more time on meaningful work.

---

# Build for Decades

Technology changes.

Models change.

Frameworks change.

Programming languages change.

The principles behind good decisions do not.

Praxis is designed as a long-term system.

Architecture should survive technology trends.

---

# Open by Design

Architecture decisions should be documented.

Important changes should begin with discussion.

The system should remain understandable.

Good architecture is a public conversation.

Not hidden implementation details.

---

# What Praxis Is Not

Praxis is not:

- another chatbot;
- another task manager;
- another note-taking application;
- another automation tool;
- another CRM.

Praxis is an operating system for decisions.

---

# Our Promise

Praxis will always strive to:

- reduce complexity rather than increase it;
- explain rather than obscure;
- assist rather than replace;
- compose rather than duplicate;
- learn rather than merely store.

---

# Guiding Principle

Everything begins with intent.

Everything meaningful ends with action.

Between those two points lies understanding.

Praxis exists to bridge that gap.

---

> **Praxis doesn't manage tasks.**

> **Praxis manages decisions.**

> **Turn intent into action.**
